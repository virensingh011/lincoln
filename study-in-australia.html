Australia at a glance

Tuition (indicative)Roughly AUD 20,000 to 50,000 a year depending on course and provider.
Living funds to showAbout AUD 29,710 a year for one student, plus tuition and travel, with traceable funds.
Student visaSubclass 500. The application charge has risen sharply in 2026, so confirm the current amount.
Main intakesFebruary/March and July; some courses also in November.
Health coverOverseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) for the whole stay.
Post-studyTemporary Graduate visa (subclass 485), usually 2 to 4 years depending on qualification.

Work and stay

Student visa holders can generally work up to 48 hours a fortnight during study, with more freedom in scheduled breaks. Indian applicants are currently assessed at the strictest evidence level, so complete and traceable documents matter.

Step by step: studying in Australia

  1. Choose course and provider

    Select a CRICOS-registered course and check it matches your background and goals.

  2. Apply and accept offer

    Pay the deposit and receive your Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE).

  3. Arrange OSHC

    Buy health cover for the full visa period.

  4. Prepare your evidence

    Gather English score, funds and a Genuine Student statement that clearly explains your plans.

  5. Lodge the visa

    Apply online, complete biometrics and health checks if asked.

  6. Plan arrival

    Arrange accommodation and travel once the visa is granted.

Common questions

How much can I work?

Up to 48 hours a fortnight during study under current rules, with more in scheduled breaks.

Can I stay after studying?

Many graduates apply for the Temporary Graduate visa. The length depends on your qualification and the rules at that time.

Why is the visa file so strict?

Australia checks that you are a genuine student with clear plans and funds. A well-prepared file makes a real difference.

Fees, funds and visa rules change often. Figures here are indicative and were last reviewed in October 2026. We confirm the latest requirements for your case before you apply, and no consultancy can guarantee a visa.
