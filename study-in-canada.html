Canada at a glance

Tuition (indicative)Roughly CAD 15,000 to 35,000 a year for most programmes; MBA and some professional courses cost more.
Living funds to showAbout CAD 22,895 a year for one student outside Quebec (from September 2025). Quebec has its own figure.
Main intakesSeptember, January and May.
English testsIELTS, PTE, TOEFL or CELPIP, depending on the institution.
PermitStudy permit from IRCC. Most applicants also need a provincial or territorial attestation letter (PAL/TAL); some groups are exempt.
Post-studyPGWP of up to 3 years depending on programme and field. Language minimum: CLB 7 for university and CLB 5 for college graduates.

Work and stay

Students can generally work off campus up to 24 hours a week during term and full time in scheduled breaks, if their permit allows it. Always check your own permit conditions.

Step by step: studying in Canada

  1. Pick course and intake

    Choose a programme that fits your background and career goal. Course relevance matters for the visa and for post-study work.

  2. Choose a DLI

    Only Designated Learning Institutions can issue a valid acceptance letter. Confirm the course is eligible for a PGWP.

  3. Apply and get your LOA

    Submit transcripts, English score, SOP and any extra documents, then receive the Letter of Acceptance.

  4. PAL/TAL and funds

    Obtain the attestation letter if required and arrange proof of funds such as a GIC, tuition payment and bank documents.

  5. Apply for the study permit

    File online with your LOA, SOP, funds proof, biometrics and medical exam if requested.

  6. Prepare to fly

    Arrange housing, insurance and travel. Your permit is confirmed at the port of entry.

Common questions

Is IELTS compulsory?

Not always for the permit itself, but most institutions need a score, and post-study work now has language minimums. We help you aim for the right score.

Can I work while studying?

Yes, within the hours allowed on your study permit. Full-time work is usually allowed in scheduled breaks.

Can I stay after my course?

Many graduates qualify for a PGWP. Eligibility depends on your institution, programme and language score.
