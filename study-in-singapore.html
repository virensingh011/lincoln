Singapore at a glance

Tuition (indicative)Varies widely by institution and course. Public universities offer a tuition grant in return for working in Singapore for a set period after graduation.
Main intakesAugust (main) and January.
English testsIELTS, TOEFL or PTE, depending on the institution.
Student’s PassYour institution applies on your behalf; you receive an in-principle approval before travel.
Work during studyRules depend on your institution and course; part-time work is limited.
After studyGraduates can seek work with the right pass, such as an Employment Pass. There is no automatic post-study work visa.

Work and stay

Check your institution’s work rules before you plan part-time work. Long-term stay after graduation depends on getting a job that qualifies for a work pass.

Common questions

Is Singapore expensive?

Living costs are higher than in many Asian cities. Tuition grants and scholarships can reduce the total.

Can I get PR?

Not automatically. Permanent residence is assessed separately and is not guaranteed.

Do I need IELTS?

Most institutions ask for an English score unless you meet their exemption rules.
