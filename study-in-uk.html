Study in United Kingdom

Why study in United Kingdom

Shorter degrees, world-renowned universities and a post-study Graduate route.

Shorter courses

Bachelor’s degrees are often 3 years and master’s degrees usually 1 year, saving time and cost.

Global reputation

UK universities are respected by employers around the world.

Graduate route

Graduates can work in the UK after study without a job offer, for a limited period.

Wide choice

Thousands of courses across business, engineering, health, law, arts and more.

United Kingdom at a glance

Tuition (indicative)Roughly GBP 12,000 to 35,000 a year; medicine and some MBAs cost more.
Maintenance fundsFrom November 2025 about GBP 1,529 a month in London (up to 9 months), lower outside London, held for 28 days, plus tuition due.
Main intakesSeptember (main) and January.
English testsIELTS, PTE or TOEFL, depending on the university and visa route.
Visa costsStudent visa fee about GBP 558 (April 2026) plus Immigration Health Surcharge of GBP 1,035 for each year.
Post-studyGraduate route: 2 years for applications up to 31 December 2026; 18 months for applications from 1 January 2027 (3 years for PhD).

Work and stay

Degree-level students can generally work up to 20 hours a week during term and full time in vacations, as stated on the visa. Only PhD and research students can usually bring family.

Step by step: studying in United Kingdom

  1. Shortlist courses

    Compare universities by course content, fees, entry scores and location.

  2. Apply

    Undergraduate through UCAS, postgraduate directly to the university.

  3. Accept offer and pay deposit

    Meet any offer conditions, accept, and receive your CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies).

  4. Prepare funds and tests

    Hold the required maintenance funds for 28 days, arrange the English score and a TB test where required.

  5. Apply for the Student visa

    Apply online, pay the visa fee and health surcharge, and give biometrics.

  6. Plan arrival

    Arrange accommodation, travel and insurance before you fly.

Common questions

How long is a UK master’s?

Most taught master’s degrees take one year, which lowers total cost.

Can I bring my family?

Under current rules only PhD and research postgraduate students can usually bring dependants.

What changes for the Graduate route?

Applications from 1 January 2027 get 18 months instead of 2 years. Timing of your graduation matters, so we plan it with you.

Fees, funds and visa rules change often. Figures here are indicative and were last reviewed in October 2026. We confirm the latest requirements for your case before you apply, and no consultancy can guarantee a visa.
