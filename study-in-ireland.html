Ireland at a glance

Tuition (indicative)Roughly EUR 10,000 to 30,000 a year depending on course; medicine costs more.
Funds to showAbout EUR 10,000 per year for living costs, plus proof of fees and private medical insurance.
Main intakesSeptember and January.
VisaStudy visa for Indian applicants, then registration for Stamp 2 permission after arrival.
Course must be eligibleChoose a course on the official eligible list so you can work part time.
Post-studyStamp 1G: 12 months for a bachelor’s (Level 8), up to 24 months for master’s and PhD.

Work and stay

With Stamp 2, students can work up to 20 hours a week in term and up to 40 hours a week in holiday periods (1 June to 30 September and 15 December to 15 January).

Step by step: studying in Ireland

  1. Choose an eligible course

    Check that the course is on the official eligible programmes list.

  2. Apply and accept offer

    Pay fees as the college requires.

  3. Prepare funds and insurance

    Arrange bank evidence and private medical insurance.

  4. Apply for the study visa

    File online, give biometrics and submit documents.

  5. Register in Ireland

    After arrival, register for your residence permit and Stamp 2 permission.

Common questions

Can I work part time?

Yes, on an eligible course: 20 hours a week in term and 40 hours in holiday periods.

How long can I stay after graduating?

Eligible graduates can apply for Stamp 1G, usually 12 to 24 months depending on level.

Do I need health insurance?

Yes, private medical insurance is required for the study visa.
